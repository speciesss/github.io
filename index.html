About

Onur Yüce Gün, PhD is an architect and computational design lead whose work spans design, computation, research, technology, and artificial intelligence.

As Global Director of Computational Design at New Balance, he leads the development of computational methods, systems, and new ways of making.

His practice has evolved across more than two decades of industry and academia, grounded in a PhD in Computation from MIT and an enduring interest in the relationships between parts, systems, and the whole.
